WHAT IT IS

An intent layer that sits on your store
and helps each visitor over the line.

One script, no theme edits. Grothfy watches how people actually move through a product page — and steps in only when it matters.

Reads real behavior

Scroll speed, price re-checks, cursor dwell — the small signals humans give off right before they decide to leave or buy.

Names the objection

Not just "at risk" — Grothfy classifies the exact hesitation: price, shipping cost, trust, or fit, so the response actually fits the doubt.

Acts in the moment

The right nudge — a capped discount, a trust badge, a size guide — fires inside the hesitation window, not five minutes later in an email.

HOW IT WORKS

Four quiet steps.
One smarter store.

Track

One script installs like a pixel. It watches scroll, dwell, price checks and cart events — nothing else.

Understand

A model reads the pattern in real time and names the specific hesitation — not just "at risk."

Decide

Grothfy picks the smallest effective nudge for that visitor — a discount, a badge, a guide — never the same offer twice.

Act

It surfaces the right message while attention is still on the page — a single-use code, applied automatically at checkout.
